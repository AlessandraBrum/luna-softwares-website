Magic Luna

A free image and PDF converter for Windows. Convert files between formats such as PNG, JPG, WebP, and AVIF. Processing runs offline on your computer, so your files stay on your device.

Explore Magic Luna Open source · Magic Luna

What's next

For the next version, I plan to add an image resizing tool and an option to choose the export DPI. I'm still looking into how to build these features and learning more about Python, the language Magic Luna is written in. I'm working on other projects as well, so the next release may take a little while.

Grimório Noturno

A free web app for looking up and understanding Magic: The Gathering cards. You can type the card name, search by voice, or scan the card with your camera. The app can read card details aloud and is designed to support players with low vision, color vision deficiency, or dyslexia.
